#### 📈 Under the Hood
- Upgraded dependencies for better performance and stability.

### Version 2.2.0

#### 📋 Smart Paste & Quick Clear
Tired of typing or copying details piece by piece? The new Smart Paste feature automatically detects and parses whatever is in your clipboard!
- **URLs** are routed to the URL input.
- **Phone numbers** are routed to the Phone field.
- **Usernames** (starting with `@`) go straight to the Username field.
- **Hashtags** (starting with `#`) are extracted automatically.
- Other text goes straight to the Message text area.

Use the **Clear All** button next to it to wipe all inputs instantly.

#### 📇 vCard Generation
Generate and download vCard contact files to easily share your contact info!
**How to use:** Tap the **vCard** platform, fill in your details, and tap the download or share buttons to distribute your contact card.

![vCard Generation](https://linkify.shahadat.us/screenshots/images/screenshot_messages_full.png)

#### 🔗 Custom Link Overrides
Need more flexibility? You can now override default links with your own custom URLs for any platform.
**How to use:** Tap the edit (pencil) icon on any active profile link to type in a custom URL instead of the default formatting.

![Custom Link Overrides](https://linkify.shahadat.us/screenshots/images/screenshot_profiles.png)

#### ✨ Cleaner UI Experience
We've cleaned up the interface! Top-level actions are now neatly tucked away in a dropdown menu to give you more screen space.
**How to use:** Tap the **More (⋮)** menu in the top right corner to access presets, manage platforms, and view the changelog.

![More Page](https://linkify.shahadat.us/screenshots/images/screenshot_more.png)
![Manage Platforms](https://linkify.shahadat.us/screenshots/images/screenshot_manage_platforms.png)

### Version 2.1.0

#### 💾 Presets & Templates
Save your frequently used handles, URLs, and messaging text as presets so you don't have to re-type them.
**How to use:** Open the **More (⋮)** menu, select **Presets**, and create a new template with your favorite links for quick access.

![Presets](https://linkify.shahadat.us/screenshots/images/screenshot_presets.png)
![Messages](https://linkify.shahadat.us/screenshots/images/screenshot_messages.png)

#### 📋 Copy All Links
Grab all your active links at once!
**How to use:** Tap the new **Copy All** shortcut at the top of your active profiles list to copy all of them to your clipboard in one go.
