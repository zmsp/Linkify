Welcome to Linkify! This guide explains how to get the most out of the application.

Overview

Linkify allows you to generate robust, shareable HTTPS links and QR codes for various social platforms. These links are perfect for embedding in emails, websites, or sharing directly with clients.

User Guide

1. Generating a Message Link

  • Navigate to the Embed Messages tab.
  • Enter the required details (e.g., Phone number for WhatsApp, Text for pre-filled messages).
  • Select a platform from the "Popular Message Platforms" or "All Messaging Apps" section.
  • Instantly preview the generated link.
  • Use the Open, Share, Copy, or QR buttons to distribute the link.

2. Generating a Profile Link

  • Navigate to the Social Profile tab.
  • Enter your username or handle for the respective platform.
  • Filter or select your desired account type (e.g., Instagram, Twitter, TikTok).
  • The link is generated automatically based on your input.

3. QR Codes

Every generated link can be converted into a QR code. Simply tap the QR button next to any platform to view and scan the code.

4. Tips & Tricks

  • All Apps Mode: By default, Linkify can generate links for all platforms simultaneously when appropriate.
  • Auto-Save: You don't need to manually save your inputs. They are persisted automatically so you can resume your session anytime.
  • Dark Mode: Toggle the theme using the icon in the top right for comfortable viewing in low-light environments.
